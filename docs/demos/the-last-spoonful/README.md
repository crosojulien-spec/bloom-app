# The Last Spoonful

**A 40th birthday between China and Prague.**

**[Open the 11-slide presentation (PDF)](BlooM%20-%20The%20Last%20Spoonful%20-%20Presentation.pdf)** · **[Visit BlooM](https://bloommm.fr)** · **[Back to the project](../../../README.md)**

A guest wants a dinner that brings back memories of China and includes his Czech friends. Follow how BlooM helps turn that wish into ideas for the food, its presentation and the service.

**Real app, fictional dinner.** Captured on 6 October 2026. The application generated the suggestions; chef decisions and prices were simulated. No restaurant has approved or served this dinner.

## 01 · Start with what matters to the guest

He is celebrating his 40th birthday with his wife and two friends. He remembers eating hong shao rou with his wife in China, especially sharing the last spoonful of sauce.

BlooM asks what he loved about it and what he wants from this evening.

![Start with what matters to the guest](screenshots/01%20-%20A%20memory%20worth%20sharing.jpg)

*Live app capture. The recap was manually corrected in this demo.*

## 02 · Make it personal beyond the food

Their Czech friends often make plum desserts. He would like a small nod to that, alongside the memories of China.

It could come through in the food, the way dishes are served or a small gesture at the table. He wants a relaxed evening, without a big birthday announcement.

![Make it personal beyond the food](screenshots/02%20-%20Beyond%20the%20ingredients.jpg)

## 03 · Use what the restaurant already does

BlooM checks the request against the restaurant’s profile: its cooking, ingredients, dishes and limits.

Here, pork and Chinese home cooking provide a starting point for the main course. The oolong cheesecake offers a base for dessert ideas. The team still needs to confirm ingredients, preparation and any special service.

![Use what the restaurant already does](screenshots/14%20-%20Restaurant%20DNA.jpg)

*English reading view of the saved restaurant profile, prepared for this demo.*

## 04 · Suggest a dish beyond the menu

BlooM suggests a “Hong Shao Rou Memory Bowl”, along with other options.

Pork fits the restaurant’s cooking. The chef still needs to check whether pork belly is available and whether there is time to prepare it.

![Suggest a dish beyond the menu](screenshots/05%20-%20Off-menu%2C%20subject%20to%20the%20chef.jpg)

## 05 · Suggest how to share it

One dessert idea combines plum, sesame and cheesecake crumbs in a cup to share.

The plum recalls the friends’ desserts. Four spoons turn it into a small moment together. This idea came from BlooM during the demo.

![Suggest how to share it](screenshots/06%20-%20Four%20spoons%2C%20one%20shared%20memory.jpg)

## 06 · Let the chef decide

The chef can accept, change or remove suggestions and set the prices.

In this demo, we keep the cucumber starter and remove the cabbage option. We also simulate approval of the pork and desserts.

![Let the chef decide](screenshots/07%20-%20The%20chef%20decides.jpg)

*These are test decisions, not approval from the restaurant.*

## 07 · Let the guest choose

The guest chooses cucumber, the pork bowl and the shared plum dessert.

The options reflect the chef’s review. The guest then confirms the menu in the app.

![Let the guest choose](screenshots/09%20-%20The%20guest%20chooses.jpg)

*The €35 shown is a demo price, not a restaurant quote.*

## 08 · Give the kitchen clear suggestions

The Chef pack suggests serving the pork in a deep bowl, with enough sauce for that last spoonful. The dessert could come in one larger bowl for everyone to share.

It explains the link to the guest’s story and lists what the kitchen needs to check.

![Give the kitchen clear suggestions](screenshots/11%20-%20Plating%20the%20last%20spoonful.jpg)

## 09 · Help the service team carry it through

The Service pack suggests placing dishes in the centre and keeping the meal relaxed.

If it suits the team, a short introduction could explain the China and Prague references or invite the guests to share the last spoonful. Special gestures still need the restaurant’s agreement.

![Help the service team carry it through](screenshots/12%20-%20Service%20brings%20the%20memory%20to%20the%20table.jpg)

## A meal that tells your story

The guest brings a story, a memory or a moment they want to share with others. The chef and the team bring the skills, ideas and care to make it part of the meal.

BlooM helps them create it together: through the dishes, the way they are presented, and the small touches that make people feel welcome.

For the guest, it is a way to share something personal around the table. For the restaurant, it opens up more of what its people can do, beyond the dishes listed on a menu.

**The guest’s story, brought to life by the restaurant’s craft.**
